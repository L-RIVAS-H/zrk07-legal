## What the game stores

The game uses your chosen pilot name or nickname, character/companion selection, language, colors, difficulty, audio and control preferences, progress and gameplay statistics locally. These personalize the experience, retain settings and support progression. You may use a nickname; playing does not require your real name, email address, password or a developer-operated server account.

The reviewed itch.io editions contain no AdMob SDK, advertising identifiers, developer-operated analytics server or global leaderboard. Pilot registration creates a local profile. Statistics are not automatically sent to the developer. The Android builds do not request Internet permission; this does not describe the independent activities of the store, system, browser or speech services.

## Where data is stored

Android and Windows saves and their recovery copies remain in local application storage. There is no developer-operated synchronization or automatic transfer between devices or platforms.

Data remains until deleted or removed by the system. Available game options let you edit profile details and preferences; for complete removal, clear application storage on Android or the demo data folder on Windows, including recovery copies. Uninstalling may erase progress. The developer cannot remotely delete a save held only on your device. Your itch.io account and purchase history are managed separately by the store.

## Speech, browser and providers

The game includes synthesized recordings and may ask the system or browser to read certain text, including your greeting nickname. The selected voice may use a local or remote service depending on its provider and device settings. That service may process the text under its own terms. You can disable voices in game settings without blocking progression.

itch.io, your browser, operating system and payment providers may process technical or purchase information under their own policies. The notices page is hosted on GitHub Pages. Its provider may receive connection data under its policy. This page includes no developer-operated analytics, forms, advertising or tracking scripts; this does not imply absence of processing by the host, itch.io or other providers.
